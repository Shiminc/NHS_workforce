Who works in NHS for our health?
Their role and where they are from

NHS is one of the biggest employers in the UK and plays an important role in ensuring all of us in the UK is well and sound. Most of the time, when using the service of the health systems, we might be interacting with clinical staff and admin staff, and not knowing what their roles are in the system. Meanwhile, there are also other staff working behind-the-scenes. So it would be worth getting to know who are working in the NHS to support our health to better appreciate them.
